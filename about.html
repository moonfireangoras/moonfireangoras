Our Philosophy

Health & wellbeing first.

The health and wellbeing of our rabbits is at the heart of everything we do. Our English Angoras are family-raised in enrichment-focused habitats with thoughtful attention to their individual needs, socialization, activity, and quality of life.

Our program focuses on health, sound temperament, and adherence to the English Angora breed standard. Thoughtful decisions are made with the welfare of our rabbits and future generations in mind.
